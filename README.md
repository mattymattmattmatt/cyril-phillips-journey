# Cyril Roy Phillips, SX8216: An Interactive War Journey

Source material for an interactive educational site that follows Cyril's WWII service (2nd AIF, 1940–1944): Adelaide, the Middle East, the Siege of Tobruk, Greece, Ceylon and New Guinea.

`source/` holds the researched family booklet (HTML chapters + PDF), his enlistment portrait (original and colourised), service record pages (NAA B883 SX8216), unit war diary pages (AWM52) and public-domain AWM photographs.
